## Shared Patterns

### Exact authorization before mutation

**Sources:** [`scripts/configure-solidstats-memory-client.py`](../../../scripts/configure-solidstats-memory-client.py:592), [`scripts/probe-solidstats-memory.py`](../../../scripts/probe-solidstats-memory.py:504)

Validate complete registration/token subtrees and exact predecessor policy before publication. Validate exact drawer ID, expected wing/room, and non-archive state before update. Reconcile uncertainty with deterministic inventory and exact reads; never guess.

### Atomicity, compensation, and prestate

**Sources:** [`scripts/configure-solidstats-memory-client.py`](../../../scripts/configure-solidstats-memory-client.py:58), [`scripts/configure-solidstats-memory-client.py`](../../../scripts/configure-solidstats-memory-client.py:235)

Use lock, no-follow regular-file checks, exclusive writes, rename exchange, inode/byte read-back, and exact accepted-state rollback. Refuse compensation that could clobber unrelated edits.

No Kubernetes, nginx, Qdrant, MemPalace server, VPS, backup, restore, reboot, cutover, or runtime rollback pattern applies. Phase 21.1 is only a machine-local Codex client policy, a fresh-session proof, exact-ID curation, and cleanup/archival evidence. Do not create or modify runtime manifests or deploy scripts.
